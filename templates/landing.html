One investigation engine, every evidence type

FORENSIQ runs the forensic checks an investigator would — content, authentication, origin, integrity and cross-source correlation — on emails, images, documents and chat/SMS exports alike, and merges the results into one case file instead of a dozen separate tools.

Cross-evidence correlation graph

Every analyzed email and every uploaded item feeds one entity graph — a sender, domain, IP or phone number shared across an email and an unrelated screenshot surfaces automatically as a correlation point.

Blockchain-anchored chain of custody

Every piece of evidence gets a SHA-256 hash the moment it's ingested, chained into an append-only ledger with optional on-chain anchoring, so integrity holds up under scrutiny.

Image tamper & authenticity check

Error Level Analysis plus EXIF/XMP-consistency checks flag recompression artifacts, design-tool fingerprints and metadata mismatches on any uploaded image or email attachment.

AI-generated image detection

Checks image metadata for embedded generation provenance (Stable Diffusion/ComfyUI prompt data, C2PA content-source declarations, known generator tool tags) plus a supplementary noise-texture signal — flagged as AI-generated only when real provenance evidence is found, not on the weak signal alone.

PDF & Word tamper check

Creation/modification-date mismatches, known editing-tool fingerprints and incremental-save detection flag a document that's been altered after it was first produced.

Explainable AI content classifier

A trained model reads for phishing language, urgency cues and credential-harvesting patterns — across email subject/body and SMS/chat evidence text — and shows the exact words that drove each verdict, not just a score.

Live web corroboration

Cross-checks a domain or phone number against the live web — not just a static snapshot — surfacing real-time scam reports with citation links an analyst can hand to a case file.

Sandboxed link preview

Visits a suspicious link inside an isolated browser — never the investigator's real IP — and reports in plain language what it does, whether it asks for a password or one-time code, and where it ends up after redirects.

Brand vs. official-site verification

Name a brand and paste a link — get one plain-language verdict on whether it's really that company's site. Built for non-technical and elderly users to self-check a suspicious message in seconds.

SPF / DKIM / DMARC authentication

Parses Authentication-Results and Received headers to verify the sender is who the domain says it is, and flags a missing or failing policy.

GeoIP, WHOIS & brand mismatch

Traces the originating IP to a country, ISP and ASN, checks domain registration age, and flags it when a "local bank" email actually originates from unrelated infrastructure abroad.

Blacklist, PhishTank & AbuseIPDB cross-check

Checks the originating IP and domain against your own blacklist, PhishTank, and AbuseIPDB reports before scoring the case.

Attachment & URL risk scanning

Flags dangerous or double extensions, macro-enabled Office files and MIME-type mismatches on every attachment, and screens links against known shorteners and brand-typosquat patterns.

Campaign clustering & anomaly detection

Groups cases and evidence items that share an IP, ASN, sender domain or phone number into a campaign, and flags statistical volume or timing anomalies against each entity's own history — a repeat number across two chat-log uploads can trigger this with no email involved at all.
