Everything an analyst checks by hand, run automatically

ThreatForensicAI runs the same checks a SOC analyst would — content, authentication, origin, reputation and infrastructure — and merges the results into one score, so triage takes seconds instead of a spreadsheet of tabs.

AI content classifier

A trained model reads subject and body for phishing language, urgency cues and credential-harvesting patterns, and returns a probability score.

SPF / DKIM / DMARC authentication

Parses Authentication-Results and Received headers to verify the sender is who the domain says it is, and flags a missing or failing policy.

GeoIP + brand mismatch

Traces the originating IP to a country, ISP and ASN, then flags it when a "local bank" email actually originates from unrelated infrastructure abroad.

WHOIS domain-age check

Newly registered sender domains are a strong phishing signal — WHOIS lookups surface registrar and creation date automatically.

Blacklist & AbuseIPDB cross-check

Checks the originating IP and domain against your own blacklist and AbuseIPDB reports before scoring the case.

Repeat-offender clustering

Groups cases that share an IP, ASN or sender domain, so a coordinated campaign surfaces even when each email looked unrelated on its own.
